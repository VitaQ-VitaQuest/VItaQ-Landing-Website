Build the programme once

The schedule is a living board, not a document. Lay out heats, finals and ceremonies across days and venues, drag a session to move it, and the whole event reflows around it — rosters, officials and families included.

  • Sessions with real timing — every heat carries an explicit start, end and venue.
  • Recurring series & exceptions — daily practice all week, a different start on finals day.
  • Officials assignment — judges and staff attached per session, notified on every change.
  • Drag to reschedule — move a heat and everything downstream updates in one action.
