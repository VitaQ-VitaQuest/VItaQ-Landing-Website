Multi-day programmes

Recurring session blocks lay out every camp day — morning ice, strength, technique, recovery — across a week or a whole season, with exceptions for the days that differ.

Enrollment & capacity

Public self-enrollment and admin enrollment feed live capacity meters on every block — watch each week fill and open a waitlist the moment it's full.

Coach assignment

Per-camp payments

Each camp pays its own way

Every enrollment ends in a real checkout — card, cash, bank or wallet — and rolls into the camp's own orders and financial summary, with pay links emailed for offline signups.

Every camp day, planned

A camp is a programme, and the programme lives on a board: session blocks per day with real start and end times, locations and coaches. Drag a block to a different day and every camper, parent and coach gets the update in their app.

  • Daily schedules — morning ice to evening recovery, each block timed and located.
  • Recurring blocks — build the pattern once, repeat it across the camp.
  • Coach per block — assignments travel with the session, notifications included.
  • Live attendance — enrollment counts on every block, every day.

Camp finances, settled at checkout

Each camp is its own financial unit. Enrollments become orders, orders become a summary, and the summary tells you exactly how the camp is doing — deposits, balances and refunds included.

  • Real checkout per enrollment — Stripe, cash, bank transfer or wallet balance.
  • Promo codes & taxes — early-bird camp rates apply themselves, taxes are calculated automatically.
  • Pay links by email — enroll a camper at the desk, let the family pay from their phone.
  • Per-camp summaries — every camp reconciles on its own ledger.
