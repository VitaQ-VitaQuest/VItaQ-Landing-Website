What accreditation will do for your event

Credential passes

One pass will carry every role and zone for the whole event

Designed to issue athlete, coach, judge, media and medical credentials straight from the records already in VitaQ.

Role-based zones

Colour-coded access, defined once, recognised at every checkpoint

Field of play, warm-up, judges' room and mixed zone — each role will map to exactly the zones it needs.

Badge printing

Print-ready badges with photo, role and QR in one click

Layouts are being designed to pull names and photos from the athlete profiles that exist in VitaQ today.

QR check-in

A scan at the door will confirm validity and zone rights instantly

Designed to give organizers live arrival counts per zone and per session as credentials scan in.
