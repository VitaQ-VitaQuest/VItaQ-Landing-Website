Run on live rails today

A championship runs on four core jobs: register athletes and collect fees, publish a programme that keeps up with the day, move people between venues, and rank performances the moment they happen. Those are the live modules — the heart of event operations, already in production.

  • Registration + fees — self-serve entries end in a real checkout, paid on the spot.
  • Schedule — drag a heat; rosters, officials and families are notified.
  • Transport — routes, drivers, vehicles and onboard manifests.
  • Standings — group-evaluation leaderboards with your scoring equations.

Championship questions, answered

Five event modules are live in production: registration & entry fees (public self-enrollment, Stripe/cash/bank/wallet payments, promo codes, taxes and a full refund workflow), event schedules (recurring series, exceptions, officials assignment and change notifications), transportation (route drafts, driver and vehicle assignment, trips with onboard manifests), training camps, and live standings via group-evaluation leaderboards with custom scoring equations.

Ticketing & seating, accreditation badges, accommodation management, tournament brackets/draws and a dedicated championship entity are in active design — not yet built. The preview pages on this site are clearly marked Coming soon, and we're recruiting design partners to shape them.

Yes. Public self-enrollment ends in a real checkout: Stripe card payments, cash, bank transfer or wallet balance, with promo codes and discounts applied and taxes handled. For offline registrations, public order and payment links are sent by email so anyone can pay from their phone.

Every change to a session — time, venue or assigned officials — triggers change notifications to enrolled athletes, their guardians and assigned staff automatically. The programme in everyone's app is always the current one, so every team, official and family sees the latest version instantly.

Yes. A programme is built from sessions, and every session carries its own start, end and location/branch. Run Friday qualifications at the practice rink and Sunday finals at the main arena inside one schedule, with live enrollment counts on every heat.
