Every entry, an order

Every registration is an order in a real finance system. Each entry carries its amount, payment method, status and history, and rolls up into financial summaries your treasurer can sign off with confidence.

  • Orders & invoices for every entry — paid, pending or refunded, never ambiguous.
  • Public pay links by email — register someone at the desk, let them pay from their phone.
  • Taxes handled automatically on every checkout and discount.
  • Financial summaries that reconcile entries against money collected.

Withdrawals, handled gracefully

Athletes get injured, teams pull out, categories merge. When it happens, the refund workflow moves each case from request to approval to capture, with every step recorded.

  • Request → approve → capture — every refund follows a recorded workflow.
  • Refund to wallet — keep the value in your ecosystem as credit for the next event.
  • Refund to card when the family prefers the money back.
  • Quotas stay honest — withdrawn spots reopen for the waitlist.
