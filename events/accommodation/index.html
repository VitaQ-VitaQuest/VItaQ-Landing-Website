Built on live rails

Accommodation isn't starting from zero. The teams it will house are already registered in VitaQ, the buses that move them already run on it, and payments clear through it every day.

  • Event registration Live — team and athlete entries with full rosters.

  • Payments & checkout Live — Stripe, cash, bank transfer and wallet with receipts.
  • Athlete CRM Live — profiles, guardians and staff records per delegation.

Accommodation will add hotels, rooms and rooming lists on top — same records, same trips, zero new tools.

Accommodation questions, answered honestly

No — accommodation is a roadmap preview, and this page shows what we're designing. What is live today: event registration with team rosters, transportation with hotel pickups and onboard manifests, and full payments (Stripe, cash, bank transfer, wallet). To help shape accommodation, join the design-partner waitlist via the demo form on our homepage.

We don't promise dates. Accommodation is in active design, and design partners get it first — they shape hotel blocks, the rooming board and hotel-ready exports with us before any wider release.

That's the core of the design. A delegation registers for the event once, and accommodation is being designed to read that roster directly — room requests, night counts and rooming lists will come from entries that already exist in VitaQ.

On rails that are live today. VitaQ transportation already runs hotel pickups with routes, drivers, vehicles and onboard attendance manifests — accommodation is being designed to plug room assignments into those same trips.

The payment engine is live today — Stripe, cash, bank transfer and wallet, with receipts, promo codes and refunds. Accommodation is being designed so lodging fees can flow through the same checkout and ledger as entry fees.
