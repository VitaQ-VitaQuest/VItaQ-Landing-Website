From requests to routes

Teams and families request their seats once, inside the app — and the planner does the heavy lifting from there.

  • Transport requests — families and teams file them in the app, in a couple of taps.
  • Route-planner drafts — requests become draft routes you review, adjust and approve.
  • Drivers & vehicles — assign both to every trip from one screen.
  • Transportation dashboard — every trip, driver and vehicle across the event in a single view.

The driver's day, scripted

Drivers don't need the whole platform — they need today's trips, the manifest and a start button. That's exactly what they get.

  • Start / end trips — one tap opens the manifest, one tap closes the trip.
  • Onboard attendance — marked at the bus door, synced to the same participation records the club and coaches see.
  • Notes per athlete — instructions ride with the manifest, visible at every stop.
  • Mileage & trip notes — logged when the trip closes, ready for reporting.
