Times that tell a story

A 500m time means nothing in isolation — it matters against last month, last season and the rest of the squad. VitaQ turns every recorded split into structured data your whole program can build on.

  • Exercise parameters for splits, lap counts and distances — with unit conversion built in.
  • Snapshots over time — every evaluation preserves the numbers, so season charts draw themselves.
  • Custom scoring equations convert raw times into points, so a 12-year-old and a senior can be ranked fairly on the same board.
  • Live leaderboards during time trials — instant competitive energy on the ice.
Skater Progress Live app component

Train long track, short track and dryland in one plan

A speed program lives in three places at once: the oval, the short track and the gym. VitaQ holds the whole block in one schedule — recurring series, assigned coaches, enrolled skaters — and when ice time moves, everyone knows instantly.

  • Recurring blocks with clean exceptions — detach one occurrence, keep the series intact.
  • Staff assignment so the sprint coach, distance coach and S&C coach each see their own day.
  • Self-enrollment and access rules — open sessions fill themselves; elite ice stays elite.
  • Attendance check-in/out with verdicts that flow straight into each skater's record.
