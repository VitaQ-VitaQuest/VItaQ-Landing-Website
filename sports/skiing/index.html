Race prep you can replay

A good training day produces twenty runs of data. VitaQ keeps every gate session as structured numbers your coaches can compare, rank and build on — all season long.

  • Exercise parameters for run times, gates skied, splits and video flags — with unit conversion built in.
  • Evaluations rank the squad — time trials become live leaderboards, scored by your own custom equations.
  • Goals per athlete per discipline — separate targets for slalom and GS, tracked side by side.
  • Season history — compare today's GS runs with October's camp, athlete by athlete.

The mountain schedule that flexes

Fog rolls in, the piste closes, the lift queue doubles — weather happens. In VitaQ you move the block, and every athlete, parent and coach knows before they reach the gondola.

  • Drag-and-drop reschedules — move a session and notifications go out instantly.
  • Recurring blocks with exceptions — shift one storm-day session without touching the series.
  • Staff assignment — the slalom coach, the GS coach and the dryland coach each see their own day.
  • Attendance with verdicts — know who made the early lift, logged straight to each athlete's record.
