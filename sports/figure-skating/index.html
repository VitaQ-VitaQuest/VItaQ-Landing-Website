Your test structure, digitized

VitaQ's stages are a one-to-one analog for skating test levels. Recreate your progression — pre-bronze, bronze, silver, gold or whichever system your club follows — and let every skater's climb track itself.

  • Stages as levels — your full curriculum, from first glide to the top of the ladder.

  • Evaluation templates per test — build the assessment once, conduct it live, publish results instantly.
