Your Philosophy, Digitized

Stop managing your curriculum with scattered documents. Our Stages feature creates a clear, hierarchical pathway for progression.

  • Define Clear Progression: Create unlimited hierarchical levels, from foundational skills to professional-tier competencies.
  • Centralize Resources: Assign specific training materials, videos, and benchmark goals to each unique stage.
  • Track Cohort Progress: Get an instant, high-level view of how groups and individuals are performing within each stage.

Your IP, Centralized

Your drills and objectives are your intellectual property. VitaQ provides a central, secure hub to house them, complete with detailed instructions, rich media, and quantifiable parameters.

  • Rich Media Integration: Add detailed descriptions, embed images, and link directly to YouTube or Vimeo videos.
  • Quantifiable Parameters: Define what success looks like by setting parameters like `Time`, `Distance`, or `Reps`.

Precision on the Field

Our Evaluation Engine transforms chaotic assessment days into models of efficiency. The live Evaluation Conductor provides a seamless on-field experience, capturing every data point with a simple tap.

  • The Live Conductor UI: See a real-time "story view" of all participants, tracking who has completed each exercise.
  • Dynamic Data Entry: The app automatically generates the correct input form—be it a timer, a numeric stepper, or a slider.
  • Robust State Management: Pause and resume a session hours or days later without losing a single piece of data.

From Data to Decisions

VitaQ turns raw performance data into your most powerful strategic asset. Make smarter coaching interventions and justify roster decisions with objective proof.

  • Trainee Analytics: Track an individual's long-term progress and compare their performance against stage benchmarks.
  • Group Analytics: Analyze team cohesion and compare performance across different squads.
  • Organizational Dashboards: Get a high-level overview of the entire academy's health.
