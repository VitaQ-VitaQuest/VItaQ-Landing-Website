A national curriculum, digitized

Define the pathway once — stages, goals, exercises and how they're scored — and every member club tests athletes against the same standard, with results that roll up nationally.

  • Stages & levels map your national curriculum into a pathway every club follows.
  • Evaluation templates standardize testing — the same exercises, scored the same way, everywhere.
  • Custom scoring equations encode your methodology so a Level 4 pass means the same thing in every rink.
  • Terminology customization — the platform speaks your federation's language, from "grades" to "patches".
Federation Analytics Live app component

Federation-grade governance

Run the platform like a governing body, not a tenant. Host-level roles keep federation oversight separate from club operations, and every club's data stays in its own walled garden.

  • Multi-tenant isolation — every club is its own tenant; data never bleeds between them.
  • Host-level roles for federation staff, distinct from club-level admins and coaches.
  • Data export & import — onboard a club from its old system, or hand its data back, any time.
  • Marketplace — member clubs are discoverable in one place, under your federation's umbrella.
