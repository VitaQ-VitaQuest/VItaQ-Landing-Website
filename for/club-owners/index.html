Capacity that fills itself in

Every program, session and camp reports its own occupancy in real time. You see which blocks are full, which have open spots, and where demand says your next program should go.

  • Live capacity meters — enrollment against capacity for every group and camp.
  • Demand signals — full programs and waitlists show you where to add sessions.
  • Booking flow built in — open spots are bookable and payable by families directly in the app.
  • Branch-level view — roll occupancy up across locations while keeping each branch's detail.
