Both kids. One tap apart.

VitaQ family accounts link every child to one parent login. Switch context in a tap and the whole app — schedule, progress, payments, attendance — becomes that child's world.

  • One login, many children — no second account, no shared passwords, no logging in and out.
  • Per-child everything — enrollments, attendance, goals and evaluation results, kept separate and clear.
  • Self-enrollment — book the right session for the right child straight from the club's public schedule.
  • Goals you can watch move — progress and rankings update as coaches record them.

Pay the way you live

Pay from anywhere: the club sends a secure payment link to your email, or you pay in the app — and the receipt, the roster and the family record all update by themselves.

  • Pay links by email — settle an invoice in two taps from any device.
  • Card, wallet or cash — Stripe-powered card payments, a prepaid family wallet, or cash recorded at the desk.
  • Refunds that make sense — refunds can go straight back to your wallet as credit for the next term.
  • Discounts & promo codes applied automatically at checkout.

Know the bus arrived

When your club runs transportation, you see it all live. Request a seat, see the assigned trip and vehicle, and watch each stop complete as the driver moves through the route.

  • Transport requests — ask for pickup from the app when the club offers it.
  • Live trip status — every stop updates as the driver progresses the route.
  • Onboard attendance — drivers check athletes in as they board, so you know your child is on the bus.
