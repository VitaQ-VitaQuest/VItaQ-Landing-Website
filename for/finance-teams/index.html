Every payment path covered

Families pay the way they actually pay: Stripe cards online, bank transfers, cash at the desk, prepaid wallets topped up in advance. VitaQ treats them all as first-class payments on the same order.

  • Stripe card payments with retries and dispute tracking built in.
  • Manual receipts for cash and bank transfers — recorded, not remembered.
  • Prepaid wallets & top-ups so families pay once and spend down.
  • Public pay links by email for self-serve settlement, no login required.

Refunds with a full audit trail
